# Asset Generation Prompts — CyberWar Tower Defense

**Art Direction:** Heavy cyber-war aesthetic. Dark backgrounds (near-black, #0a0a1a). Neon accent lighting in blues, purples, reds, and greens. Circuit-board textures, holographic overlays, military hardware aesthetics, glowing energy cores. All units should feel like advanced military technology in a near-future digital warzone. Pixel art or stylized 2D game art (top-down or slight 3/4 isometric perspective preferred). No photorealism — clean, readable silhouettes for a tower defense game grid.

## NOTES FOR AI IMAGE GENERATION

- **Style keywords to include in all prompts:** `pixel art`, `top-down view`, `game sprite`, `transparent background`, `cyber war`, `neon lighting`, `dark background`
- **Avoid:** Photorealistic, 3D renders, white backgrounds, single-character DPI overshooting the tile size
- **Recommended tools:** Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion (with pixel art LoRA), Adobe Firefly
- **For animated GIFs:** Use Aseprite or Piskel to animate 4–8 frame loops; import into `public/assets/gifs/` and register in `src/config/visualAssets.ts`
- **File naming convention:** `tower_basic_rifle.gif`, `enemy_bug.gif`, `enemy_juggernaut_boss.gif`
- **Grid size reference:** Tower tile = 64×64px (game CELL_SIZE), enemies = 48×48px sprite within tile
